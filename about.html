About Mzansi Radio Waves

Mzansi Radio Waves was built for every South African who loves radio - from taxi drivers and students to gogos at home. We bring together SABC, commercial and community stations in one place.

We link directly to official station streams. We do not host any audio streams ourselves.

Based in Soweto, Gauteng. Made for Mzansi.
